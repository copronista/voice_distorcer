Voice Distorcer records a voice message, anonymizes it on the device, and
shares the result through the Android Sharesheet.

The app applies pitch and formant shifting plus a set of fun effects so the
speaker is no longer recognizable. All processing happens locally. The app
has no network access and uploads nothing.

The full build supports recording audio or video, importing media, previewing
the processed clip, applying presets, fine-tuning DSP parameters, and backing
up settings.

The full build requests RECORD_AUDIO and CAMERA only for local recording.
Files shared with other apps use a content URI and a random 12-character
suffix in the filename.
